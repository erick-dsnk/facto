Reading… Searching… Weighing sources…

Two checks, in the lens

Someone nearby says “Goldfish only remember things for three seconds.” Facto searches Live Science, BBC and National Geographic and shows: False, 3 of 3 sources disagree. Live Science: “Goldfish can remember things for months, not seconds.”

You say “Facto, can you check this?” while looking at a post claiming the Eiffel Tower grows up to 15 cm taller in summer. Facto reads the screen, searches the official site and Britannica, and shows: True. Source: toureiffel.paris.

Two ways to check

It listens.

When someone near you states a fact, Facto checks it on its own.

You ask.

Say “Facto, can you check this?” and it reads what you’re looking at: a phone, a screen, a poster.

One word in the lens, the details on your phone.
