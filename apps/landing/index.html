04 / A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

Good
questions.

Curiosity looks good on you.

What can De Facto check?
Public factual claims that can be compared with evidence: a statement on a slide, something in the news, or a claim in conversation. Opinions and personal experiences aren’t treated as facts to correct.
Does it always have an answer?
No, and that matters. Some claims need more context; others can’t be verified with the available evidence. De Facto makes that uncertainty clear instead of forcing a verdict.
Which devices does it work with?
De Facto brings Meta glasses and your iPhone together. Your glasses capture the moment, spoken results keep you present, and your iPhone gives you the full evidence and source links.
Is it always listening?
No. You choose when to capture an image or start a listening session. Stop whenever you want. You’re in control of when De Facto joins the conversation.
Does it tell me who’s telling the truth?
De Facto checks claims against sources. It doesn’t judge a person’s intentions. A useful piece of context should open up a conversation, not shut it down.
