# Prospective OPEN full-context scope/stance contrasts v1

This separate design has 12 synthetic contexts in six paired families, three records per context. It contains no record-capability receipt labels, real factual outcomes, authentic-source claims or measured factual-coverage credit. No candidate appendix/code, new model output, network source or known failed broader case was read to select the controls. The author has public-development and generic mechanism familiarity; this is OPEN authoring, not a blinded accuracy benchmark.

The neutral second-labeling packet is `annotation-inputs.json` plus `rubric.md`. Keep `labels-author.json` and authoring scripts separate until independent second labels freeze. The exact `proposition` and complete `records` are inference inputs. Case/category/stratum, author rationales, labels and contrast intent are analysis metadata and must not enter a provider prompt. All initial assessment fields use the same neutral placeholders, irrespective of their expected outputs.

Six paired families test: missing career ordinal versus a retained explicit ordinal; locality versus an in-body city mapping; signing versus entry into force; incomplete eligibility condition versus a full same-rule exception; metadata title versus in-body record fields; and in-scope number/date/negation counterexamples. A complete companion contains precisely the information missing from the paired incomplete target. The target must be assessed from its own passage rather than borrowing companion contents. Wrong compared values can create contradiction without a scope mismatch. No model answer is predicted as a result of changing the candidate wording.

The primary denominator is 12 target records in 12 declared contexts, not 36 independent facts. All 36 retained record assessments are also labeled and scored separately; repeated companions and paired contexts are correlated. There are 24 unique record IDs/content fingerprints. The final pair keeps both targets in scope while changing the count/stance. SC12 deliberately retains a contrary same-period audit companion; no target-level scope/stance result may suppress that conflict or supply a definitive full-pool result.

Synthetic entity names, publishers, dates, events and `.invalid` URLs are fixture data. `retrievedAt` is a scenario clock, not evidence of a fetch; no source is official or independently verified. The corpus is for scope/stance assessment calibration only. Source content basis, risk, confidence, identity/authority, dependence, record capability, final verdict and presentation remain separate. It cannot justify a real-world truth claim or production activation.

`labels-author.json` covers all 36 record occurrences, including the 12 primary targets, with exact quoted spans and material-role rationales. Uncertain labels are permitted by the rubric and must remain visible if reviewers identify them. Author labels remain unchanged after outputs; independent labels/adjudication belong in separate dated artifacts. No filtering, target substitution, rerun or inferred label is authorized by this design. A separately reviewed execution plan must bind frozen candidate/request bytes, budgets, orders, deadlines, all declared safety diagnostics and provider usage before calls. The real runtime deadline must not be replaced by the synthetic scenario clock.

`build.py` and `finalize-draft.py` are offline authoring provenance, not runnable provider experiments. Do not rerun them into this frozen folder. Preserve original artifacts; use a new version for corrections. The applicability36 corpus remains separate and unchanged.
