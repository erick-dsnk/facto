# Prospective direct scope/stance rubric

These twelve full contexts are wholly synthetic OPEN controls, not factual accuracy cases. Read only annotation-inputs.json and this rubric for independent second labels. Do not read author labels, authoring scripts, candidate appendix/code or provider outputs before freezing labels. No real institution, source authority, outcome or web retrieval is asserted by the invented records. The scenario retrieval timestamp is synthetic metadata only.

Label each retained record against its context's exact proposition. Required fields are caseId, evidenceId, expectedScope (true/false/null), expectedStance (supports/contradicts/unrelated/null), a quoted supporting span and rationale, ambiguity/exclusion if needed. The marked target is the primary contrast; companion records are also scored separately. Keep uncertainty explicit rather than forcing a label.

Scope asks whether that individual retained passage addresses the material relation, referent and circumstances needed to support or refute the exact proposition. A different compared value, date or negated assertion does not by itself put a complete same-relation passage out of scope: it can be contrary evidence. A different event (signing versus entry into force), award series, reporting period, population or unresolved geographic level can be material mismatch. Explain a date's role as event value or reporting period. Scope and stance are separate from source content basis, authority and record-capability applicability.

Inspect each passage independently within the full context. Do not lend a missing ordinal, place mapping, operative condition or event date from a complete companion to an incomplete target. A title or metadata year cannot fill absent in-body fields. A fragment ending at an operative condition remains incomplete. In-body headings/labels and their actual associated values can constitute complete record contents; prose narration is not required.

First/career-wide ordinals need an explicit ordinal or sufficient earlier history in the retained record; a recipient/year alone does not complete that historical universe. City membership needs a retained association, not world knowledge about a ward/province. A conditional rule with an explicit in-scope counterexample can refute a universal all claim. Do not suppress such a counterexample because it disagrees with the claim, and do not manufacture the omitted condition when the target fragment lacks it.

Stance is support or contradiction of the whole exact proposition by that passage, not lexical agreement or a final verdict. A complete same-period record reporting 17 refutes not 17; another complete record reporting 19 can support not 17. A different reporting year is not another compared value for the same period. Incomplete or different-relation passages may be related background without supporting/refuting the whole proposition; use unrelated for that three-way stance label when appropriate. Use null for genuine unresolved ambiguity and document why.

Keep all retained contrary evidence visible. A target's correct scope/stance cannot delete another record or authorize a definitive whole-pool result when credible conflict remains. No factual verdict, confidence threshold, capability receipt or final notification decision is assigned here. Report target-only and all-record scores with their distinct denominators and correlated pairs. Do not include category/stratum, labels, rationales or author intent in provider inference inputs.
