The cost is a concrete false-rejection diagnostic. BD10's Federal Register excerpt says the institution “was created by an act of Congress on August 10, 1846,” with its statutory citation. Its original-order scope falls .92 → .81 while contradiction stays 1.00 and basis stays .91. House history falls .93 → .72; the two Smithsonian accounts fall .92/.93 → .83/.84. In reversed order, House history falls .92 → .78. These passages address the same founding relation with a different asserted legal mechanism. The independent selected-source audit had already marked their retained propositions complete. The candidate therefore fails to preserve the value-disagreement boundary it was meant to retain.

The two Baikal passages still pass in reversed order at candidate scope .90 each, with contradiction .99 and qualifying content-basis assessments. Their retained region/country/coordinates do not explicitly supply the continent relation. The previous independent source audit documents this limitation; no new geographic lookup or source substitution was made here. A correct real-world verdict does not repair incomplete retained justification.

There are no new candidate definitive outputs in the existing stratum. Final verdict labels change in two of twelve paired conditions, both BD10 contradiction-to-abstention. Three policy selections change, including BD03's narrower selected set. This is sufficient to reject promotion without interpreting the synthetic controls as a general accuracy estimate.
