# Frozen baseline: full context, reversed order and isolated passage

The diagnostic finds context-sensitive scores, but **no full-context factual
coverage gain and no proven ID-binding defect**. No alias, prompt or production
code changed. It does not authorize isolating passages in production.

Six fixed OPEN contexts were evaluated once under each condition: the exact
candidate08 final full set in original order, that entire set reversed, and the
target passage alone. All full-set companions, copied/origin bridges and potential
conflicts remained. The actual frozen08 source commit was
`aefb9e7c5f0f3d0e332eea5d8748878b3f6cf75c`. Conditions occupied first/middle/last
execution slots twice each. BD11's middle target remains at index2 when its
five-item set reverses; this was declared before execution.

Two independent label sets were frozen before dispatch. Five primary targets
have agreed binary content-basis labels: four substantive and one insufficient.
The chronology is excluded as a boundary, with both views retained; its recorded
biography does not independently settle the exact first-Physics ordinal. Formal
record genre is not authority/authenticity or proof of all claim qualifiers.

At the unchanged .85 accepted-class gate, primary positive passage recall is
2/4 in both full orders and 3/4 in isolation; the one negative is rejected in all
three conditions. No primary target changes pass/fail between the two full
orders. The UNESCO target changes from `bare_assertion` in both full orders to

pass used a different excerpt, so that earlier-to-final change cannot be
attributed to load.

Historical scores are a separate requery comparison: none of the 31 retained
records changes its quality-gate result between the saved historical judgment
and this run's new original-order full judgment. The within-new-run comparisons
are more informative than treating a fresh call as a fix. Even they change
several things at once: passage count, question count and often target position.
UNESCO is at index0 in reversed full and isolation, yet its scores/genre differ;
target alias position alone does not explain that contrast. One call per
condition cannot distinguish stochastic variation, semantic contrast, question
load and imperfect reference resolution.

All 18 mechanical binding checks pass: returned source-assessment and diagnostic
keys match exactly the supplied retained IDs; selected IDs match the validated
wire alias/index mapping; every full graph has the required valid origin pairs.
No source ID was invented or lost. This verifies code-level mapping, not that
the model always reasons about the intended passage. An explicit questionKey
alias has not been tested or promoted.

All 25 non-target retained records are compared canonically in `comparison.json`.
Fresh original versus reversed full sets produce one non-target quality-gate
change: BD10's Smithsonian history page falls from .88 to .80. Nine other
non-target records cross a stance-probability, relevance or scope gate across
the two orders. Origin-choice/confidence changes, risk confidence, raw verdict
probabilities, selected citations and every per-ID old/new value are preserved.
The full policy labels remain the same in both orders: the two positive/negative
fact controls keep their prior definitive labels; the other four contexts
abstain. Citation sets/order can differ. Every isolated policy result abstains.
These are diagnostic policy outputs, not newly verified factual accuracy.

Full-claim caveats remain material: UNTC conclusion and UNTS signature fields
must not silently become adoption evidence; ceremony/year does not supply an
unstated first-Physics ordinal. The excluded chronology remains a safety sentinel
for any future full-context comparison, not a passage whose quality promotion
can automatically earn coverage credit.

started fetches, 0 Exa, 160,349 measured input tokens, all 18 calls reporting

This deliberately selected, tiny OPEN sample cannot establish population
calibration, an alias fix, end-to-end usefulness or production readiness. Keep
all protected gates and all companions. Any new full-context treatment requires
another fixed plan and independent pre-dispatch review; this result authorizes
no replay or production change.
