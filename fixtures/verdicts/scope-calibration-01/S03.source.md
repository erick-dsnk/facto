Aconcagua, the highest mountain in the Americas, Argentina. (more)

Mount Aconcagua, mountain in western Mendoza province, west-central Argentina, on the Chilean border. It is the highest point in the Western Hemisphere.

Aconcagua lies in the Southern Andes Mountains; although its peak is in Argentina, its western flanks build up from the coastal lowlands of Chile, just north of Santiago. Its name possibly originated from the Quechua Ackon Cahuak (“Sentinel of Stone”). Aconcagua
