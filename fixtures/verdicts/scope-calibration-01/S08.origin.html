Suez Canal

How old is the canal ?

​The canal has been opened for navigation for the first time in Nov. 1869.


What is the depth of the canal ?

​Maximum water depth is 24 m.


What is the length of the Canal ?

​The length of the canal is 193.30 Km.


What is the width of the canal ?

​The width of the water surface is 313 m. The width of the navigational channel is between 200m and 210 m.


Who owns the canal ?

​The canal is owned completely by the Egyptian government. No shares are available in the stock market.


How long does it take to a vessel to transit the canal ?

​It takes 12 to 16 hours.


How many days per year the canal is available for navigation ?

​The canal is available for navigation all over the year.


How many times the canal was closed for navigation ?

​It was closed for 6 times. The shortest period was 3 days.The longest period was 8 years (following the 1967 war).


Shall a pilot be on board of each vessel to transit the canal ?

​Yes, the pilot on board of each vessel is compulsory (except for small fleets).


Suez Canal Authority

Are there any foreign employees in Suez Canal Authority ?

​No, all employees are Egyptians.


How many employees are there in the Suez Canal Authority ?

​There are about 14000 employees.
