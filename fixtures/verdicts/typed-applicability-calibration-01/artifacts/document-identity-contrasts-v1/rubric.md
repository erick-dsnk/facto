# Trusted document identity versus operative facts

Use only annotation-inputs.json, sources.json and this rubric for independent initial labels. Do not read labels-author.json, build.py, candidate adapter02 wording/code or provider results. This is a separate prospective OPEN packet, not a blind factual evaluation or permission to activate a receipt.

Assume the named descriptor is supplied only after an independently reviewed admin-installed receipt and matching private canonical snapshot are verified. That assumption identifies the audited canonical document/version/class and its dated-snapshot mode. It does not convert a caller/source string into a trusted handle. Forged or mislabeled handles are separate deterministic negatives and are not counted as learned semantic performance here.

Every retained excerpt is the exact complete audited unit from an already captured public record. Assess the same bounded applicability question as in the original design: permitted relation, referent and material circumstances, independent of agreement with the asserted value. An operative clause can say this document without repeating its RFC number or version; a trusted descriptor may resolve that record referent. The actual asserted field, value/condition and their association must still occur in the retained unit itself. A different compared value can be covered when the corresponding record field is present.

Do not promote identity metadata into an answer. A date/year inside versionId does not itself establish publication, adoption, reference date, entry into force or present validity. An award category/edition identifies the entry, not a first-ever or career ordinal. A population-release identity does not fill a missing age-specific count. A dated consolidation does not prove current/effective status at a later asOf time. Preserve all meaningful relations and qualifiers rather than supplying familiar world knowledge or nearby full-body fields outside the retained unit.

Return expectedApplicability covered/not_covered/uncertain, an exact quoted supporting span, rationale, temporal reading, ambiguity/exclusion where genuine, and gating layer. Uncertain meaning remains unavailable; do not force a label to favor an adapter. Covered supplies provenance applicability only if all separate trust, risk, substance, directness, scope, stance/confidence, citation, full-pool conflict/dependence, budget and lifecycle gates pass. No descriptor raises a source-quality probability or creates an independent origin.

The prospective asOf timestamp anchors deictic date meaning. It is not a new origin-access timestamp, effective-version proof or runtime deadline. Saved source access/lineage remain original. Full-context companion evidence must remain visible but cannot lend an absent operative fact to the target. Applicability is neither a direct-scope/stance label nor final factual-verdict eligibility.
