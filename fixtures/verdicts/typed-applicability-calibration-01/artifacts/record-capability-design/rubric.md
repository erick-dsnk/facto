A receipt is independently reviewed server-side provenance data. Retrieved text, self-declared headers, metadata titles and claimed digest strings cannot create or alter it. The packet contains proposed receipts and public proof objects for independent review; these are not production trust grants. Evaluate their actual issuer/remit, canonical identity, inspected bytes, record locator, version and limitations rather than assuming the proposal is valid. If issuer identity/competence evidence is inadequate, flag the affected units rather than silently treating the host as authoritative.

Applicability is independent of agreement. An authentic record can be applicable when it refutes the asserted value. Conversely, a familiar true statement, a publisher's home/about page or a copied title cannot inherit a document-specific capability. Distinguish a record heading plus its actual fields from a heading or citation without contents. Require the actual retained passage to align with the audited record unit; arbitrary material elsewhere in the same body has no inherited competence.

A trusted local hash of preserved canonical bytes may reuse the original attestation for its pinned historical version, with the original access time and one origin. An untrusted mirror's self-reported matching hash is not that trusted observation. Do not invent a new access or count copied/translated/syndicated material as independent corroboration. In this corpus there are no actual external mirror fetches; such cases are explicit controls.

Match record and event semantics. An award's year does not establish the work's publication year or an ordinal. A normative definition does not establish implementation compliance. A legislative rule does not establish its empirical effect. A reported rounded population estimate is bounded by release, geography, reference date, population and measurement definition; it is neither a universal exact count nor the latest/current value merely because the issuer is official. Correctly scoped older records remain usable historically; stale or different records cannot inherit another version's receipt.

A valid receipt can satisfy a source-qualification route only. All existing risk, conflict, directness, scope, stance, confidence, citation and budget checks remain separate. If the control context supplies a credible contrary passage, preserve the conflict veto even when the target receipt applies. Do not infer that such a simulation describes a real source conflict. No receipt stores an expected answer or verdict.
