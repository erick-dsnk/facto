**Text:** The sans-serif big-word title always sits center. Captions are not burned in. Product text is the UI itself. The logo is built from the mascot's eye shape and ends on a plain black dot plus wordmark.

**What does the work:** (1) mascots make a software product feel like characters, (2) one micro-story (headphone launch) shown through five tiny interactions, ending on a visible win.

**Steal:**
1. Give your agent or product one tiny recurring character or icon that reacts in every scene. Remotion circle plus two eyes.
2. Show the product through one concrete story with a payoff ("campaign is live", reaction count climbing).
3. Kinetic-type zoom: the spoken sentence becomes one huge word that scales to the frame, then docks into the UI.
4. One pastel or color background per feature so scenes change without extra cuts.
5. Fade audio in from silence in the first 3 s, and end on a near-silent logo hold.

The scroll-stopper is the instant relatable scenario plus pace: a cut or glitch every 0.1-1.5 s (cuts at 2.57, 2.67, 2.77 are three flash frames).

**Beats:**

**Pacing:** 14 detected cuts all inside the first 14.3 s (about 1 s average, with four-cut bursts at 8.4-8.7), then three calm sections of 6-12 s. The video accelerates through the problem list and then relaxes for the demo. Pixel-block dissolve transitions between the phone shots.

**Look:** Matte off-white, dithered or halftone gradients (blue/green), then black with a warm orange-red orb. Premium and editorial, mostly neutral with one warm accent.

**Text:** Small light sans-serif, centered, typed in word by word and scattered around cutouts. White on black at the end. The logo is small.

**Sound (inferred):** Loud from the start (-11 LUFS) with the narrator over what is probably a music bed. **Steal:**

4. Play real product audio as chat bubbles, not a screen recording.
5. Keep the video to 30-35 s for a social cut of our 2-minute film.
