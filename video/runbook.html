00:10 · Omni

Gemini Omni: what it can do for us

We cannot edit our own footage with Omni from Finland.

Google's Omni docs list editing and extending uploaded video as unavailable in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK. Editing videos Omni generated itself still works. Do not plan on "add a hologram to our real clip" through Gemini.

Use it for

  • Text-to-video hero shots: the hook, the problem visual, the transition, the outro
  • Image-to-video from a ZV-1 still with no people in it (laptop, desk, hall)
  • Multi-turn edits of a clip Omni generated

Specs to plan around

  • 3–15 s per clip, 720p native (1080p/4K are upscales), 16:9 or 9:16
  • About 24 fps [third-party], so conform to 25
  • Generates its own audio. We strip it.
  • API exists: gemini-omni-1.2-flash, about $0.07/s at 720p [third-party price]
  • App limits reset roughly every 5 h [unverified]. Check the usage meter.

If we really want an element added into real footage

Test once, on a shot with no faces (a laptop close-up), through Higgsfield, which is already connected to Claude. In an independent six-model test, Runway Aleph 2 was the most reliable at adding objects with correct shadows. Omni Flash tended to regenerate the whole scene. Kling O1 is the backup. If the test looks off, fake the effect with a Remotion overlay. Never run AI edits on talking-head shots. Faces drift.

What this changes in our plan
  • Captions are the main text layer. Neither video uses boxed pop-up cards. Our default is Farza-style centred captions, with only 3–5 real pop-ups (one stat, the logo, the architecture).
  • The "AI animation" is the product itself, moving over real UI. If our product has any visual element, animate that in Remotion over zoomed screen footage.
  • Film the laptop screen with the ZV-1, close up with shallow focus, as well as recording it. It is the cheapest cinematic shot in both videos.
  • Cut every 1.5 s in montage sections. Talking lines run longer, but punch in or change angle at least every 3 s.
  • Proof shots work: real screenshots, real numbers, real people reacting.
  • End on personality: a loose CTA and a blooper make it human. Judges and peers remember it.
  • Omni hero shots play the part of the archive and desert cutaways: 1–2 s metaphor shots, never the main content.

Demo plus tech get about 50 seconds. Judges score what the product does, so the cinematic work goes around the demo and the demo itself stays plain and readable.

Cheap tricks that read as cinematic

  • Punch-in: scale 1.00 → 1.08 over 6 frames, landed on a whoosh, at every new talking line
  • J and L cuts: next shot's audio starts 0.5 s before its picture
  • Silence drop: 0.3–0.5 s of nothing before the title or key line
  • Speed ramp 100% → 30% → 100% on b-roll actions
  • One LUT, grain and vignette over everything, AI clips included, so it looks like one film
00:45 · Pipeline

Five stages, five gates

Claude does the work in each stage. A human approves at the gate before the next stage starts. Feedback format: s3@0:04 cut 0.5s, s2 swap take B, popup2 bigger.

G1

Script

Claude draftsTeam approves

About 240–280 spoken words in 6 beats. Every line is tagged [TALK:name], [DEMO], [HERO:prompt] or [POPUP:type]. Claude also writes shots.md and a prompter.html teleprompter (large text, speed slider) to read from a laptop under the lens, one scene per take.

Pass when everyone can say every line out loud without tripping.

G2

Ingest, transcribe, pick takes

Claude runsHuman picks or swaps
mkdir -p ~/junction-video/{script,raw/{cam,screen,omni,broll},work/conform,edl,remotion,out}

# conform everything to 1080p 25 fps CFR, 48 kHz
for f in raw/{cam,screen,broll}/*; do
  ffmpeg -n -i "$f" -vf "scale=1920:1080:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,pad=1920:1080:-1:-1,fps=25,format=yuv420p" \
    -c:v h264_videotoolbox -b:v 30M -c:a aac -ar 48000 -b:a 256k "work/conform/$(basename "${f%.*}").mp4"
done

# Omni clips: 24 → 25 fps by a 4% speed-up, audio dropped
for f in raw/omni/*.mp4; do
  ffmpeg -n -i "$f" -vf "setpts=PTS*24/25,scale=1920:1080:flags=lanczos,fps=25" -an \
    -c:v libx264 -crf 14 -pix_fmt yuv420p "work/conform/omni_$(basename "$f")"
done

# word-level transcript (put product names in the prompt so Whisper spells them right)
pip install mlx-whisper rapidfuzz
mlx_whisper work/conform/take.mp4 --model mlx-community/whisper-large-v3-turbo \
  --word-timestamps True --output-format json --initial-prompt "ProductName, TeamName"

The take picker (about 40 lines of Python) scores each take's transcript against its script line with rapidfuzz, penalises long takes with restarts and writes the in/out points into edl.json. Claude shows the pick and two runner-ups per scene in a small HTML page.

01:05 · Remotion

Building the pop-up engine

npx create-video@latest            # choose Blank, TypeScript, Tailwind
cd my-video
npx remotion skills add             # official Claude Code skills; start with /remotion-best-practices
npm i @remotion/transitions @remotion/captions @remotion/media-utils \
      @remotion/google-fonts @remotion/motion-blur @remotion/paths
npm run dev                          # Studio on localhost:3000 for scrubbing and prop edits

Remotion 4.0.543 is current. Pin every @remotion/* package to the same version. The licence is free for organisations of up to 3 people, so check the licence page if the team is bigger. Set both compositions to fps: 25.

Component set

  • Kinetic keyword: per-word spring up with blur 12px → 0
  • Stat counter: 0 → value over 30 frames, tabular numbers
  • UI card: glass card slides 80px in with a slight 3D tilt
  • Callout: circle scales in, line draws, label types on
  • Lower third: bar wipe, name and role stagger in
  • Split screen: A-roll shrinks aside, demo fills the rest
  • Centred captions, 2–4 words at a time, bold white with a soft shadow (the default text layer)
  • Zoomed UI: screen footage pushed in 2–4× with blurred edges
  • Full-screen title card for chapter breaks

Motion and finish

  • Entrances 12–20 frames, spring({damping:200}). Exits are 6–8 frame fades.
  • Hold each pop-up 2–3.5 s. One accent colour only.
  • Transitions: mostly hard cuts. Whip pan = slide + camera motion blur.
  • Grain loop at 12% overlay, radial vignette, letterbox on hero shots only
  • Whoosh 3 frames before each cut, music at 0.12 ducked under voice
  • Use OffthreadVideo, never Video
  • Demo zoom: record with Screen Studio (trial) or Cap (free), both auto-zoom on clicks
01:20 · Shoot

Camera, sound and b-roll

Settings were researched from memory and not checked against the ZV-1 manual. Confirm menu names on the camera.

ZV-1 settings

  • NTSC/PAL selector set to PAL, then XAVC S HD 1080/25p 50M
  • M mode, shutter 1/50, widest aperture (f/1.8 at 24 mm)
  • Auto ISO capped at 3200, ND off indoors
  • Picture profile off, Standard, contrast −1. Skip S-Log.
  • Custom white balance on a white sheet under the real light
  • AF-C, Face/Eye AF on, Product Showcase off, Background Defocus on
  • Auto power-off temp: High. Spare NP-BX1 or USB-C power bank.

Where and how

  • Quiet side room, dark background, a lamp or monitor glow behind you
  • Light the face from 45° with a window (sunset about 15:45) or a laptop showing a white page
  • Mic 15–25 cm from the mouth, peaks around −12 dB
  • Voice Memos backup on a phone, one clap per take, 10 s of room tone
  • Hook and closing line to the lens. Everything else slightly off-lens, interview style.
  • Clean up audio: DeepFilterNet locally, or Adobe Podcast Enhance mixed 70/30 with the original

B-roll in 20 minutes (S&Q 100 fps for slow motion)

Hands on keyboard lit by the screen · screen glow on a face in the dark · whiteboard marker close-up · fist bump · can cracking open · sticky-note wall slide · hall wide from the stairs · cables and hardware · timer close-up · finger hitting Enter on deploy. The clean, people-free shots double as image-to-video inputs for Omni.

Screen demo

Display at 1920×1080, Do Not Disturb on, fresh Chrome profile, Dock hidden, larger cursor, demo data pre-filled. Write the 5–7 click path, rehearse once, record 2–3 takes, and pause for a second before and after every action so the edit can speed-ramp.

01:35 · Prompts

Omni hero shot prompts

End each one with: Single continuous shot, no cuts, 16:9, cinematic, diegetic sound only. Swap in our real problem and product.

Hook · text to video

Slow dolly-in on a dark hackathon hall at 3am, hundreds of laptops glowing, one developer's screen floods with red error text. Shallow depth of field, teal-and-amber grade.

Problem · text to video

Overhead orbit around two developers at one desk, their code drawn as glowing threads that collide and tangle above the table, sparks where they cross. Moody, low-key light.

Product comes alive · image to video

Using the reference image as the first frame, a translucent holographic dashboard rises from the laptop screen and floats above the keyboard. Slow push-in. Keep the laptop, desk and lighting identical. Text on the hologram stays soft and unreadable.

Transition · text to video

Camera flies into a laptop's glowing screen, through a tunnel of data streams, and emerges as a wide shot of a city skyline at dawn. One continuous move.

Outro · text to video

Slow pull-back from a team silhouetted against a large window at sunrise, lens flare, dust drifting in the light, calm and hopeful. Clear dark space on the right for a logo.

Rules

Use 3–4 s from each clip, the cleanest part. Put any readable text in Remotion, never in the prompt. Grade and grain hero shots with the rest of the footage.

01:58 · Open items

Check before relying on it

  • Confirm the camera model (ZV-1, ZV-1 II or ZV-E10) and that the 1080/25p option shows after switching to PAL
  • Try one text-to-video prompt in the Gemini app today to see quality, fps and whether a visible watermark appears
  • Check Junction's submission rules: file or link, size cap, exact deadline
  • If testing an AI insert, check in Higgsfield which of Runway Aleph 2 and Kling O1 are available, and the credit cost
  • Decide whether our hook is a character story (X reference) or a founder talking to camera with proof (Instagram reference). For a 2-minute judged video, founder plus proof gets to the product faster.
